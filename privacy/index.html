Privacy Policy

Effective 29 September 2026

In short: Fishoto does not collect your personal data. There is no account, no analytics, no ads and no tracking. Your catches are stored on your device and in your own iCloud, and we cannot see them.

Who we are

Fishoto is developed by an independent developer based in Poland. For any privacy question, write to fishoto.contact@gmail.com.

What the app stores

When you add a catch, Fishoto saves what you enter: the photos you choose, species, length, weight, date and time, the fishing spot (name and location), method, lure and notes. This data is stored on your device and synced through the private iCloud database of your Apple Account, so it is available on your other devices. It never reaches us. iCloud is provided by Apple under Apple's terms and privacy policy.

Photos

You choose photos with the system photo picker, and Fishoto receives only the photos you select. The rest of your library stays hidden from the app. If you save a post image, Fishoto asks for permission to add it to your Photos.

Location

With your permission, Fishoto uses your location only while you add a fishing spot, to show where you are on the map. The coordinates of a spot are saved only when you save that spot. Place search and address lookup are provided by Apple Maps.

Captions

On supported devices, post captions are written by Apple Intelligence on your device. The text is not sent to us.

Purchases

Fishoto Pro subscriptions are sold and billed by Apple. We never receive your payment details. Apple gives us only aggregated sales reports that do not identify you.

What we don't do

  • We don't use analytics, advertising or tracking tools.
  • We don't include third-party SDKs.
  • We don't sell or share any data.

Children

Fishoto does not collect personal data from anyone, including children.

Your control over your data

You can delete any catch in the app. To remove everything, delete Fishoto from your devices and delete its data in your iCloud storage settings. Because we hold no copy of your data, there is nothing for us to delete on our side. If you have a request under the GDPR or any other privacy law, contact us at the address above.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new effective date.

Что хранит приложение

Когда ты добавляешь улов, Fishoto сохраняет то, что ты вводишь: выбранные фото, вид, длину, вес, дату и время, место ловли (название и координаты), способ ловли, приманку и заметки. Ты выбираешь фото через системное окно выбора, и Fishoto получает только выбранные фото. Остальная медиатека остаётся скрытой от приложения. Когда ты сохраняешь изображение поста, Fishoto просит разрешение добавить его в Фото.
